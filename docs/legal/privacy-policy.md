## 1. Who we are

Lunair World is a product of **Wershuffle Inc** ("we", "us", "our"), a United States
corporation. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use the
Lunair World website, application, alerts, and newsletter (the "Service"), how we use
it, and the choices you have.

Questions, or want to exercise a privacy right? Email **guy@wershuffle.com**.

## 2. Information we collect

**You give us:**

| What | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Email address | Account signup, newsletter subscription | Sign-in, alerts, newsletter, support |
| Name and business name | Account setup (optional) | Addressing you correctly, invoices |
| Product information - descriptions, materials, country of origin, whether a product has a battery or plug, intended audience, product photos, listing links, and optionally an approximate annual import value | Product Passport | This is the core of the Service: it is what we match against public government publications to tell you which requirements appear to relate to your products |
| Classification codes you select | Product Passport | Monitoring the codes you chose |
| Telegram chat identifier | Only if you connect Telegram | Delivering alerts on that channel |
| Support messages and assistant conversations | When you contact us or use the in-product assistant | Answering you; improving our help content |
| Feedback, ratings, and survey responses | When you give them | Improving the Service |

**We collect automatically:**

- **Usage data** - pages viewed, features used, alerts opened, approximate time and
  session information.
- **Device and connection data** - browser type, operating system, and IP address. We
  use IP address for security, fraud prevention, and approximate country-level
  location; we do not use it to pinpoint you.
- **Email engagement** - whether an alert or newsletter was opened and which links were
  clicked, in aggregate counts.

**We receive from others:**

- **Stripe** tells us your subscription status, plan, and the last four digits and brand
  of your payment card. **We never see or store your full card number.**
- **Public web pages** you ask us to fetch, when you paste a product listing link.

**We do not collect** government identification numbers, financial account numbers, or
health information, and we ask you not to submit them.

## 3. How we use information

- To provide the Service: build your product profiles, match them against public
  government publications, and send you alerts, digests, and status updates.
- To operate your account and process payments through Stripe.
- To send the newsletter you subscribed to, and service messages about your account.
- To answer support requests and power the in-product assistant.
- To improve the Service, including reviewing low-rated AI answers so we can correct
  them, and analyzing which alerts users find useful.
- To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse, and enforce our Terms.
- To comply with legal obligations.

**We do not use your product information to train third-party AI models.** Our AI
provider processes your data to return a result to you and, under our agreement with
them, does not use it to train their models.

## 4. We do not sell your personal information

**We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context
behavioral advertising**, as those terms are defined under California law. We have not
done so in the preceding 12 months.

## 6. Cookies and analytics

We use cookies that are necessary for the Service to function, such as keeping you
signed in. For analytics we use Plausible, which does not use cookies and does not
track people across sites, and PostHog for product analytics within our own Service.
We do not run advertising trackers or third-party ad pixels on the application.

Our newsletter measures opens and link clicks in aggregate. We do not use tracking
pixels beyond that basic measurement.

## 7. Email choices

- **Alerts and digests** are part of the paid Service. You can control channels and
  frequency in your settings, or turn them off.
- **The newsletter** uses double opt-in: you confirm by clicking a link in a confirmation
  email before we ever send you an issue. Every issue has a one-click unsubscribe link
  and our postal address in the footer.
- **Service messages** - billing receipts, security notices, and material changes to
  these policies - are not optional while you have an account, because we need to be
  able to reach you about your account.

## 8. Your privacy rights

If you are a California resident, the CCPA as amended by the CPRA gives you the right
to: **know** what personal information we collect and how we use it; **access** a copy
of it; **correct** inaccurate information; **delete** it; **opt out** of sale or sharing
(we do neither); and **not be discriminated against** for exercising these rights. We do
not use personal information for automated decision-making that produces legal or
similarly significant effects about you.

Residents of other states with comparable laws, and users elsewhere, may exercise the
same rights - we apply them to everyone rather than sorting by geography.

**How to exercise them:** delete your account and its data yourself at any time from
your account settings, or email **guy@wershuffle.com**. We respond within 45 days and
will tell you if we need longer. We verify requests by confirming control of the account
email. You may use an authorized agent; we may ask for proof of authorization.

## 9. Deletion and retention

**Self-serve deletion.** Your account settings include a delete button that removes your
account, product profiles, alert history, and assistant conversations.

**What we keep after deletion, and why:** billing and tax records, for as long as tax and
accounting law requires; a suppression record of your email address, so we do not email
you again by mistake; and security and abuse logs for up to 12 months. Aggregated,
anonymized statistics that cannot identify you may be retained indefinitely.

**Otherwise:** we keep personal information while your account is active, and delete or
anonymize it within 90 days of account closure, subject to the exceptions above.
Backups are purged on a rolling cycle of up to 35 days.

## 10. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls, hosted infrastructure with managed
security, and error monitoring. Payment card data goes directly to Stripe and never
reaches our servers. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute
security. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and any
regulator as required by law.

## 11. International users

The Service is directed at businesses importing into the United States, and our
infrastructure is in the United States. If you use the Service from outside the United
States, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States,
where privacy laws may differ from those in your country.

## 12. Children

The Service is for business use and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not
knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given
us information, email us and we will delete it.
