# USPTO knockout search - LUNAIR / LUNAIR WORLD
_Run 22 August 2026 against the live USPTO Trademark Search index (the modern TESS
replacement), cross-verified against TSDR. Preliminary screen for the attorney to
interpret - **not a legal opinion**, and not a clearance search._

## Bottom line: clear in our classes

**There is no LUNAIR or LUNAIRE trademark of any kind in Class 35 (business services)
or Class 42 (software as a service) - not one, live or dead.** The exact phrase
"LUNAIR WORLD" has never been filed in the United States, in any class.

Wildcard, fuzzy (edit-distance-1), and phonetic searches on LUNAIR, LUNAIRE, LUNAR,
LUNA AIR and LUNARIA restricted to classes 35 and 42 all return zero records.

## Three things for the attorney to weigh

1. **The Class 9 registration deserves a second look.** Active Apparel Group's LUNAIR
   (Reg. 6429430) sits in Class 9, which also covers downloadable software, and
   examiners sometimes treat Class 9 software as related to Class 42 SaaS. Its goods
   are narrowly limited to respiratory masks, which cuts strongly in our favor, but
   it is the one record that could theoretically generate a 2(d) refusal.
2. **Others are actively claiming the name nearby.** The Global HVAC application in
   Class 11 was published for opposition in February 2026. Filing sooner protects our
   priority date in 35/42 rather than later.
3. **"WORLD" adds little distinctiveness.** For conflict purposes our real mark is
   LUNAIR. If budget allows, filing both LUNAIR and LUNAIR WORLD in classes 35 and 42
   is the stronger position.

## Method and limits

Queried: the USPTO Trademark Search backend API (roughly 20 structured queries -
phrase, wildcard, fuzzy, owner-name, pseudo-mark, class-filtered) and the TSDR API for
independent status confirmation. Justia was blocked (403) and not used.

**This screen does not cover:** state trademark registers, common-law or unregistered
use, foreign registries (EUIPO/WIPO), company-name conflicts, or the multi-factor
likelihood-of-confusion analysis an attorney applies. Index records load through July
2026, so a very recent filing may not appear.
